# Moves

The eight ways a product can grow. They name no product. Your profile says which fit yours, under **Channels**, **Moves off** and **Constraints**. To add a move, copy the five fields.

## Fix
Question: What is breaking for people now?
Fits when: people fail, stall or leave at a step you can point to.
Evidence: a step's counts, errors, complaints.
Output: code change
Not when: too few people reach the step for the problem to matter.

## Reach
Question: How do more of the right people find this?
Fits when: few arrivals, a reachable audience and a clear offer.
Evidence: arrival counts, search demand, where the audience gathers.
Output: code change or brief
Not when: the product cannot yet serve the people it would bring.

## Capture
Question: What intent already exists that we do not catch?
Fits when: people look for this, or arrive, and leave without acting.
Evidence: searches, impressions without clicks, arrivals without a next step.
Output: code change or brief
Not when: the promise would go beyond what the product holds.

## Offer
Question: Is there something new worth coming for?
Fits when: the audience has a need the product does not yet answer.
Evidence: repeated requests, questions asked, what others charge for.
Output: brief
Not when: the current offer has not been tested on enough people.

## Distribute
Question: Can we go where the audience already is?
Fits when: another channel or partner already holds these people.
Evidence: arrivals by source, channels listed in the profile.
Output: brief
Not when: the channel is outside the profile's Channels or Constraints.

## Compound
Question: What keeps paying off after it ships?
Fits when: one piece of work can bring people for months.
Evidence: lasting sources of arrivals, referrals, repeat use.
Output: code change or brief
Not when: an urgent break needs the time first.

## Prove
Question: What would make people trust this faster?
Fits when: people arrive and hesitate at the point of deciding.
Evidence: drops at the decision step, doubts people state.
Output: code change or brief
Not when: the proof would claim more than the record shows.

## Learn
Question: What do we not know that would change the plan?
Fits when: one unknown would change the choice between other moves.
Evidence: a named gap, and the decision it blocks.
Output: code change or brief
Not when: Learn won the last run, or a bet on current evidence is reversible.
