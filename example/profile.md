# Product profile

This product is invented for this example. Gourdly and all its numbers are illustrative.

**Product:** Gourdly, a shared seed and planting calendar for community gardens.

**Repo:** none, invented product

**Audience:** community garden organisers, in local growing groups and allotment forums

**Goal:** weekly sign-ups

**Channels:** search, garden forums, a seasonal email

**Moves off:** none

**Constraints:** no paid ads

**Moves file:** moves.md

**Mode:** acquisition

**Funnel steps:** arrive, sign up, add a first bed, come back within 14 days

**First moment of value:** a new account adds a first bed and sees its planting dates

**Numbers file:** example/numbers.md

**Watch:** weekly sign-ups at 40 or more

**Routing:** watch haiku, research sonnet, disprove opus, decide opus, engineer sonnet, navigator opus

**Context store:** local

**Context path:** example/product-context

**Settled:** Gourdly stays free for gardens under 20 plots. No mobile app this year.

**Rules the code must hold:** Every query on a garden's data checks membership.

**Test commands:** npm test, npx tsc --noEmit
