# Sample evidence

Invented numbers for a typical web product, used only in the first preview until real data is connected.

- 58% of visitors who start sign-up never finish it.
- 31% of new accounts never complete the first key action.
- 12% of new accounts come back in week two.
- The pricing page gets 4 times more visits than the sign-up page.
- Most support questions in the first week are about setup.
- Visitors from search convert at half the rate of visitors from referrals.
