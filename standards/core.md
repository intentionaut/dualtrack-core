# Core

Applies to every surface. Name the rule by its number in every finding.

1. **Status**: the person can tell what is happening and what just happened.
2. **Their words**: labels, messages and docs use the audience's words, not the code's.
3. **Undo and exits**: a way back, a dry run or a confirm before anything that cannot be undone.
4. **Consistency**: the same thing has the same name and behaviour everywhere.
5. **Prevent errors**: safe defaults, validation before work starts, no trap states.
6. **Recognition over recall**: the next step is shown, not remembered.
7. **Efficiency**: common paths are short; repeated work can be scripted or skipped.
8. **Less**: every element earns its place; the important thing comes first.
9. **Recover from errors**: an error says what happened, why, and the exact next step.
10. **Help where it is needed**: help sits at the moment of use, short and searchable.
11. **Accessible**: nothing depends on sight, colour, a mouse or fast reading alone.
12. **No deception**: no fake urgency (a countdown, scarcity or "others are looking" that is not true), no cancelling or deleting made harder than signing up, no data shared with anyone the person was not told about. Severity 4.

Severity, 1 to 4: 1 cosmetic, 2 minor, 3 major (people get stuck or wrong), 4 blocking
(people cannot finish or lose work).
