# Web and app screens

Numbered WEB-n. Accessibility follows WCAG 2.2 at level AA, grouped by its four principles.

**Perceivable**
1. **Contrast**: text 4.5:1, large text and UI parts 3:1.
2. **Text alternatives**: every meaningful image, icon and chart has one; decorative ones are hidden.
3. **Not colour alone**: state and meaning also show in text, shape or position.
4. **Reflow and zoom**: works at 320 CSS px wide and at 200% text size without losing content.

**Operable**
5. **Keyboard**: everything works by keyboard, in a sensible order, with no traps.
6. **Focus visible and not obscured**: focus is clear and never hidden by sticky headers or banners.
7. **Target size**: pointer targets at least 24 by 24 CSS px, or spaced to match.
8. **Dragging**: anything done by dragging has a single-pointer alternative.
9. **Motion**: respects reduced-motion settings; nothing flashes more than three times a second.

**Understandable**
10. **Labels and instructions**: every input has a visible label; no placeholder-only labels.
11. **Errors**: shown next to the field, say how to fix it, and keep what was typed.
12. **Redundant entry**: never ask for the same information twice in one task.
13. **Consistent help**: help and contact sit in the same place on every page.
14. **Accessible authentication**: no memory or puzzle test to sign in without an alternative.

**Robust**
15. **Names and roles**: custom controls expose name, role and state to assistive tech.

**States**
16. **Every state designed**: empty, loading, error, partial and success, not left blank.
