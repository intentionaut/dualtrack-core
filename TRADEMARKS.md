# Using the Dualtrack name

The Functional Source License (FSL-1.1-ALv2) lets you use, change and share Dualtrack's code. It does not cover the name. Its Trademark clause says so: it grants no rights to the maker's names, trademarks or product names.

"Dualtrack" and its mark belong to Saielle DaSilva (https://intentionaut.com).

## You can

- Use Dualtrack, and share unchanged copies under its name.
- Say your project is "based on Dualtrack" or "a fork of Dualtrack".
- Say your tool works with Dualtrack.

## You cannot

- Call a changed copy Dualtrack, or use the Dualtrack mark for it.
- Suggest it comes from Dualtrack's maker, or that the maker approves it.

Pick your own name for a changed copy. Keep `LICENSE` and `NOTICE` with it, as the licence asks.
